# Contributor Covenant Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention,
trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information,
and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct issues privately through the contact process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy fairly may be removed from
the project team.
